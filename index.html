My Vision and Mission as Alawe of Ilawe‑Ekiti

What I shall do from the Stool

Vision

To make our town Ilawe‑Ekiti united, peaceful, prosperous, culturally proud, secure and forward‑looking. To build an exemplary community where tradition and modern development work together, and where every indigene and resident has the opportunity to live with dignity, contribute meaningfully, and prosper.

Mission

To provide inclusive, visionary and purposeful traditional leadership; to preserve the heritage and dignity of the Stool of Alawe of Ilawe‑Ekiti; to unite our people; mobilise development partnerships and investments; empower our youth; protect the vulnerable; and position Ilawe‑Ekiti as a model community in Ekiti State and in Nigeria.

1

Promoting Unity, Peace and Social Harmony

As the father of all indigenes and residents of Ilawe‑Ekiti — irrespective of religion, political affiliation, social status, age, gender or place of residence — I shall provide leadership that is fair, inclusive, compassionate and impartial.

  1. Foster peace, mutual respect, understanding and harmony among all quarters, families, associations, institutions and interest groups.
  2. Promote reconciliation and healing wherever there are disagreements, divisions or grievances, recognising that the strength of Ilawe lies in the unity of its people.
  3. Build stronger bonds between Ilawe at home and Ilawe people in the diaspora, creating meaningful opportunities for our sons and daughters worldwide to contribute to the development of their ancestral home.
  4. Promote inclusive leadership and consultation, ensuring that every section of our community feels heard, respected, valued and represented.
  5. Protect the collective interest of Ilawe‑Ekiti by encouraging dialogue, fairness, due process and peaceful resolution of disputes.
  6. Give necessary attention to outstanding legal and institutional matters inherited from the previous Alawe and Alawe‑in‑Council, working with the appropriate authorities and professional advisers to seek their timely and satisfactory resolution in the best interest of Ilawe‑Ekiti.
2

Preserving Our Heritage and Cultural Identity

As custodian of the traditions, history, culture and ancestral heritage of Ilawe‑Ekiti, I shall ensure that our identity remains strong even as our community embraces the future.

  1. Protect, preserve, promote and transmit the rich cultural values and traditions of Ilawe‑Ekiti and the Yoruba race.
  2. Safeguard our historical sites, traditional institutions, festivals, landmarks, customs and cultural practices for present and future generations.
  3. Uphold the dignity, integrity, sanctity and prestige of the Alawe Stool, ensuring that the institution continues to command respect within Ilawe‑Ekiti, Ekiti State, Yorubaland and beyond.
  4. Promote the documentation of Ilawe’s history — our origins, traditions, institutions, heroes and significant historical events.
  5. Encourage younger generations to understand and value their heritage, so that modernisation does not cost us our identity and our values.
3

Championing Community Development

As Alawe I will not merely be a custodian of tradition; I will also be a catalyst for development — mobilising our people and building partnerships with government, non‑governmental organisations, the private sector, development agencies, philanthropists and our diaspora.

  1. Advocate for improved healthcare facilities, roads, potable water, electricity, telecommunications and other essential infrastructure.
  2. Reposition our traditional institutions and community leadership structures to play a more active and constructive role in development.
  3. Build stronger relationships with government agencies at Federal, State and Local Government levels, and maintain constructive engagement with all political administrations.
  4. Mobilise the Ilawe diaspora and development‑minded individuals and organisations to support sustainable projects in our community.
  5. Encourage community ownership and participation, so that development initiatives are sustainable and answer the genuine needs of our people.
  6. Protect our educational institutions.
On the Federal College of Education

The establishment of the Federal College of Education in Ilawe‑Ekiti is an important opportunity for our community. But development must not come at the expense of an existing institution that serves our children. I shall work constructively with the relevant authorities and stakeholders to ensure the Federal College of Education receives the support it needs to thrive here, and that the Ekiti State Model College, Ilawe‑Ekiti, is restored and reopened at its appropriate location — with the interests of students, parents, teachers, the host community and the wider people of Ilawe protected.

Our approach shall not be “add and drop”. It shall be “add and preserve.” We must expand educational opportunity rather than replace one valuable institution with another.

4

Driving Economic Growth, Investment and Prosperity

I envision an Ilawe‑Ekiti where our people do not merely survive, but build sustainable livelihoods, generate employment and create wealth.

  1. Modern agriculture, agro‑processing and agribusiness, leveraging the agricultural potential of our land.
  2. Small and medium‑scale enterprise, through access to finance, training, markets, technology and mentorship.
  3. Tourism and cultural enterprise, taking advantage of Ilawe’s history, culture, traditions and natural environment — Ikereje Hill, Oke‑Òrín and Ajagémò.
  4. Strategic investment partnerships capable of creating employment, generating wealth and improving the standard of living of our people.
  5. An enabling environment for entrepreneurship and innovation, particularly for young people and women.
  6. Diaspora investment — encouraging Ilawe sons and daughters around the world to see their hometown not only as a place of origin, but as a place of opportunity.
5

Advancing Education and Empowering Our Youth

The youth are not merely the leaders of tomorrow; they should be partners in building Ilawe today.

  1. Initiate and support scholarship schemes, mentorship programmes, educational grants and other initiatives that promote academic excellence.
  2. Promote vocational, technical, digital, entrepreneurial and professional skills development.
  3. Encourage our youth to pursue excellence, innovation, creativity, responsible citizenship and leadership.
  4. Create opportunities for youth participation in community leadership and development programmes.
  5. Support initiatives designed to reduce unemployment, discourage social vices and promote economic self‑reliance.
  6. Establish stronger links between our youth and successful professionals, entrepreneurs, institutions and members of the Ilawe diaspora.
6

Environmental Sustainability and Orderly Town Development

A prosperous Ilawe must also be clean, orderly, attractive and environmentally sustainable.

  1. Promote environmental cleanliness and effective waste management.
  2. Encourage proper physical planning and orderly urban development.
  3. Protect our forests, water resources and other ecological assets.
  4. Mobilise community initiatives that encourage residents to take collective responsibility for the appearance and cleanliness of our town.
7

Welfare, Inclusion and Protection of the Vulnerable

Traditional leadership carries a profound responsibility to protect the dignity and welfare of the vulnerable.

  1. Advocate for improved healthcare and social welfare services.
  2. Initiate programmes supporting women, children, widows, the elderly, persons with disabilities and other vulnerable members of our community.
  3. Protect the interests of host communities.
On the host community

Where our people have made sacrifices for the advancement of Ilawe‑Ekiti, those sacrifices must be recognised and treated with fairness. In particular, the host community that voluntarily made its land available for the Federal College of Education must not be worse off for that generosity. I shall engage the relevant authorities and stakeholders to ensure the legitimate interests and rights of the landowners are recognised, and that appropriate compensation and other benefits due to them are pursued in accordance with applicable law and agreements.

8

Security, Peace and Public Safety

No meaningful development can take place in an atmosphere of insecurity. I shall work closely with security agencies, community leaders, youth organisations, traditional institutions and residents.

  1. Strengthen peace, security and public safety throughout Ilawe‑Ekiti.
  2. Promote responsible community vigilance and crime prevention.
  3. Encourage early intervention and peaceful resolution of conflicts and disputes.
  4. Promote a culture of peace, tolerance, responsibility and respect for law and order — so that Ilawe‑Ekiti remains a safe, peaceful, welcoming and investment‑friendly community.
My Commitment to Ilawe‑Ekiti

Bridges, not walls

My aspiration is to serve as a unifying and development‑oriented Alawe — one who respects our ancestors, protects our heritage, understands the realities of the present, and prepares our community confidently for the future.

To unite, not divide Opportunity, not dependency Development, not stagnation

The strength of Ilawe‑Ekiti lies not only in the Alawe Stool, but in the collective wisdom, talent, resources, sacrifice and goodwill of our people at home and across the world. My leadership shall be founded on consultation, inclusion, fairness, integrity, collaboration and service.

We shall all build Ilawe together.
